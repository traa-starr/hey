# why hey you exists

hey you is made by Mojo, because they care. Every person who opens this app is someone carrying something, and they chose, for a moment, to be on their own side. Everything we build has to honor that choice.

This is the heart of the project. When anything in the code, content or design is unclear, start here.

## what we believe

**Every person deserves kindness, especially from themselves.** Most people talk to themselves in ways they would never talk to someone they love. We're here to help them close that gap, gently and in their own words.

**People aren't problems to solve.** No one who opens hey you is broken or doing life wrong. Their inner voice has usually been trying to protect them, so we meet it with curiosity and gratitude, not correction.

**We show; we don't scold.** It isn't enough to point out that someone is hard on themselves; they already know. We give them the felt experience of being kind to themselves, so they can feel the difference.

**Truth is part of kindness.** We never promise what the research doesn't support, never flatter, and never hand people words they can't believe. Kind words that feel true do more than big words that don't. Every claim has a source and an honest label.

**Gentleness is strength.** Soft doesn't mean small. It takes courage to be kind to yourself, and we say so.

**Peace over pressure.** No streaks, no guilt, no nudges, no points. People come back because it feels good to be here, not because we hooked them.

**Privacy is care.** What people share here is theirs alone. It stays on their phone, and nothing revealing is ever shown to someone looking over their shoulder.

## how it feels to use

Like being welcomed by someone who's glad you came:

- **Gratitude first.** We thank people for showing up, for being honest, and for spending time on themselves.
- **Light, not heavy.** We start with what's good, then gently make room for what's hard.
- **Warm and adult.** Never cute, never clinical, never preachy. Lowercase, plain, kind.
- **Every answer is welcome.** "it feels strange" and "i don't believe it" get the same warmth as "it feels lovely".
- **Always a way out.** Anything can be skipped. Nothing is ever failed.

## the questions we ask of everything we build

Before anything ships, ask:

1. Would someone who has been blamed all their life feel safe here?
2. Does it leave them a little lighter than they arrived?
3. Did we show them they can be kind to themselves, rather than tell them they've been unkind?
4. Is every word true, and could we stand behind it?
5. Did we thank them, welcome them, or appreciate them somewhere along the way?
6. Would they come back because they want to?

If the answer to any of these is no, it isn't finished.

## a note to every builder, human or not

You're building something for people on their hardest days and their ordinary ones. Write every line as if the person reading it is someone you love who's having a rough week, because someone will be. Be gentle, be truthful, be glad they're here.
