# Study plan: first round with real people

A small, safe study to learn whether hey you is clear, comfortable, and worth coming back to. Five people, remote, on their own phones, about 45 minutes each, plus an optional one-week follow-up.

## What we want to learn

1. **Clarity:** can people find their way (home, learn, practice, you) and finish a voice check, a lesson, and an answer without help?
2. **Comfort:** does anything feel judging, preachy, childish, or too revealing on a shared phone? (CLAUDE.md test 2 and test 4)
3. **Value:** after one week, does anything stick? Do people reach for a way to answer, or notice a voice habit, in real life? (test 3)
4. **Pull:** would they come back because they want to, with no nudges? (test 6)

We are not measuring wellbeing or symptoms. That would need a different study, with ethics review.

## Who

- Five adults (18+) who say they "talk to themselves harshly sometimes". Recruit through friends of friends and community boards, not clinics.
- A mix: at least two who don't use wellbeing apps, two on iPhone and two on Android, one who uses a screen reader or large text if possible, at least one aged 45+.
- **Not eligible:** anyone currently in crisis, or who tells us they would rather not talk about their inner voice. Say this kindly at screening, and offer the help lines anyway.
- **Thanks:** a gift card for the session, the same whether or not they finish.

## Safety and consent (read out at the start, and sent in writing first)

- "This is a test of the app, not of you. There are no wrong answers, and nothing you say is graded."
- "Please don't share anything you'd rather keep private. When the app asks what your inner voice says, you can use one of its ideas or make one up. You never have to type your own."
- "We won't ask about your past or anything painful. If something comes up anyway, we can pause, skip, or stop, with no reason needed. You'll still get the gift card."
- "This isn't therapy, and we aren't therapists."
- "We'd like to record the screen and audio for our notes. The recording is deleted after we write up, within 30 days. You can say no and we'll take notes instead."
- Real help: have the help lines from `content/help.json` ready to share. If someone seems to be in distress, stop the task, check in like a person, and share the lines. Don't continue the session that day.
- What's on their phone stays on their phone: the app keeps no server data. Show them how to clear it (settings → your data → clear everything) at the end if they want.

## Session guide (45 minutes)

**Warm-up (5 min)**
- Introductions, consent, recording yes or no.
- "What do you usually use your phone for, to wind down or think things through?"

**Context (5 min)**
- "Some people have a voice in their head that's hard on them. Does that sound familiar, in general terms?" No details needed; move on after one answer.

**First open (10 min).** Install from the link and open it. Think aloud.
- Watch: the welcome, naming, the choice to do a voice check or look around.
- Ask after: "What do you think this app is for?" and "Who do you think it's for?"

**Tasks (15 min).** Think aloud; don't help unless they're stuck for 60 seconds.
1. Do a voice check. Then: "What does the map tell you? Does anything feel off or judging?"
2. Do the first lesson in "meet your inner voice". Then: "Was that too long, too short, or about right? How did dawn feel?"
3. "Imagine your inner voice is loud right now. Use the app for that moment." (They can pick an idea instead of typing their own.)
4. Find your book. "What do you make of this page?"

**Reaction (7 min)**
- "If a friend picked up your phone right now, is there anything here you'd mind them seeing?"
- "Was there a moment you felt talked down to, or rushed?"
- "What, if anything, would make you open this again tomorrow?"
- One word for how it feels to use.

**Wrap-up (3 min)**
- Optional follow-up: "Would you be up for a 3-question message in a week?"
- Offer to show how to clear their data.

## One-week follow-up (optional, by message)

1. Did you open hey you again this week? What made you open it, or not?
2. Did anything from it come to mind in a real moment? (A way to answer, a habit's name, a line you kept?)
3. Anything that annoyed you?

## What we'll watch for

| signal | where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| confusion | hesitations over 5 seconds, wrong taps, "where do I…" |
| discomfort | sighs, "hm", skipping, softened or joking answers, any wish to hide the screen |
| value | spontaneous "oh, that's me", reading a line back, using a habit's name in their own words |
| pull | naming a reason to come back that isn't a reminder |
| access | zooming, screen reader trouble, misread contrast, targets that are hard to hit |

Notes go in the synthesis kit: `docs/research/synthesis-kit.md`.

## Timeline

Week 1: recruit and screen. Week 2: five sessions. Week 3: follow-ups, synthesis, and changes.
